Hello, I am Abhishek Bhowmik

Graphic Designer and Web Designer

As a UI/UX designer with 4+ years of experience, and a notable technical know-how acquired through a short stint in development, I am confident in my ability to craft exceptional digital experiences. Over the past few years at Webkul, I have consistently delivered intuitive and visually appealing designs for both customer-facing and enterprise software, specializing in eCommerce and CRM solutions. My passion for creating user-centric solutions drives me to excel in designing interfaces that seamlessly bridge the gap between functionality and aesthetics.

My design obsession knows no bounds. I'm equally fascinated by the sleek lines of industrial design, the grandeur of architecture, and the realms of 3D modeling. If it's got a design element, consider me intrigued. Above all, I appreciate the elegance of minimalism, finding beauty in clean lines and uncluttered spaces, and I strive to infuse this appreciation for simplicity into all my creative endeavours. Simplicity is hard to achieve but once it's there, it's beautiful.
