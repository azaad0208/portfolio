QuickBite

From order to delivery, in sync.

Gemini Google AI Studio ChatGPT

A real-time, full-stack restaurant management, kitchen orchestration, and on-demand delivery ecosystem. It bridges the operational gaps between front-of-house (customers/diners), back-of-house (chefs/line cooks via a dedicated Kitchen Display System — KDS), restaurant management (owners/cashiers), logistics partners (delivery riders/agents), and platform administrators.

An all-in-one, real-time restaurant operating system and delivery platform that connects diners, kitchen staff, store managers, and delivery riders into one synchronized system.

What it connects

  1. Digital Ordering (Customers)

    Browse interactive menus, add special cooking instructions, and order for Dine-in (table QR), Takeaway, or Delivery with live milestone tracking.

  2. Smart Kitchen Display System (KDS for Chefs)

    Replaces paper tickets with digital tickets featuring live urgency timers (white → yellow → red) and a Batch Prep mode to cook identical items together.

  3. Instant Front-of-House Control (Owners/Managers)

    Review incoming orders, hold or reject with reason, monitor live prep times, and bump prepared food to “Ready for Dispatch.”

  4. Live Menu & Stock Management (Kitchen Staff and Restaurant Management)

    Chefs or managers can mark any item “Out of Stock” directly from the kitchen screen, immediately hiding it from customer menus.

  5. Integrated Delivery Logistics (Riders)

    Riders get notified the second food is bagged and ready, claiming deliveries and updating status from pickup to doorstep.
